Shahid Mallya

A distinctive voice known for bringing emotion and warmth to every melody he sings.

HIS STORY

A voice that deserves to be heard.

Shahid Mallya is an Indian playback singer known for his soulful and folk-influenced vocal style. He made his Bollywood singing debut with “Gurbani” from Yamla Pagla Deewana and gained major recognition with “Rabba Main To Mar Gaya Oye” from Mausam. He has since sung for films including 2 States, Udta Punjab, Jab Harry Met Sejal and Rocky Aur Rani Kii Prem Kahaani. He was part of the award-winning album Jab Harry Met Sejal at the 2018 Mirchi Music Awards and was also nominated for a Filmfare Award for Best Male Playback Singer for “Kudmayi”

From romantic melodies to powerful compositions, his versatility has allowed him to create a unique place for himself in Indian music.

Awards/recognition:
🏆 Mirchi Music Award – Album of the Year (2018) – Jab Harry Met Sejal
🏆 Mirchi Music Award – Album of the Year (2015) – 2 States
🎵 Filmfare nomination – Best Male Playback Singer for “Kudmayi” (Rocky Aur Rani Kii Prem Kahaani)
