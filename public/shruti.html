Shruti Pathak

A powerful voice whose music has touched countless listeners, yet deserves to be discovered by many more.

HER STORY

A voice that deserves to be heard.

Shruti Pathak is an Indian playback singer known for her distinctive, expressive voice in Hindi film music. She began her professional singing career through remix albums and gained wider recognition with “Mar Jawaan” from Fashion (2008). She later lent her voice to songs such as “Rasiya” (Kurbaan), “Paayaliya” (Dev.D), “Tujhe Bhula Diya” (Anjaana Anjaani), “Shubhaarambh” (Kai Po Che!) and “Tooh” (Gori Tere Pyaar Mein). Her singing in Fashion earned her Filmfare and Screen Award nominations for Best Female Playback Singer. She has also been part of the Mirchi Music Awards jury, reflecting her standing in the Indian music industry.

From soulful melodies to energetic playback numbers, her versatility has allowed her to explore different emotions and musical styles.

Awards/recognition:
🎵 Filmfare Award nomination – Best Female Playback Singer for “Mar Jawaan” (Fashion)
🎵 Screen Award nomination – Best Female Playback Singer for “Mar Jawaan”
🎵 Mirchi Music Awards recognition – her songs “Mar Jawaan” and “Rasiya” appeared among the Female Vocalist of the Year selections.
