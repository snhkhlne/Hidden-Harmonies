ABOUT THE PROJECT

More than a music collection.

Hidden Harmonies is a digital space dedicated to discovering lesser-known artists and exploring the stories behind their music.

Stories

Every artist has a journey shaped by their experiences, influences and struggles. We look beyond the music to understand the person behind the voice.

Music

Songs are more than recordings. They carry emotions, ideas and memories. Hidden Harmonies brings attention to music that may not have reached a mainstream audience.

Discovery

Discovery is about finding artists outside familiar playlists and popular charts, allowing listeners to explore different voices and musical journeys.
