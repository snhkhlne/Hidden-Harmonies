HER STORY

A voice that deserves to be heard.

Ronkini Gupta is an acclaimed Indian Hindustani classical vocalist, playback singer, composer, and vocal coach, renowned for her pitch-perfect rendition and a unique style that fuses elements of the Gwalior and Kirana gharanas. With over 25 years of rigorous training under luminaries such as Pt. Chandrakant Apte, Ustad Dilshad Khan, Pt. Samaresh Choudhury, and the late Ustad Abdul Rashid Khan, she holds a Sangeet Visharad distinction from Gandharva Mahavidyalaya

In 2023, Gupta was honored with the Swar Kokila Lata Mangeshkar Award for her contributions to classical music.
From Bollywood songs to independent music, Ronkini has created performances that leave a lasting impression on listeners.

Awards/recognition:
🏆 Artist Aloud Award – Best Composition (2016) for Ritu Basant Ki with The Kheyaal-E-Jazz Projekt.
🏆 Artist Aloud Award – Best Hindi Song (2018) for Aye Ri Chandni with The Kheyaal-E-Jazz Projekt.
🏆 Zee Talkies – Maharashtra Cha Favourite Kon (2020) for Tula Japnaar Aahe from Khari Biscuit.
🏆 Swar Kokila Lata Mangeshkar Award (2023)
🎵 Filmfare Award nominations for Best Female Playback Singer — Rafu from Tumhari Sulu (2018) and Chaav Laaga from Sui Dhaaga (2019).
🎵 She also received All India Rank 4 in Indian Classical Vocal at the Doverlane Music Conference in 2000.
