Soham Naik

HIS STORY

A voice that deserves to be heard.

Soham Naik is a singer and independent music artist known for his soft, melodic vocal style. He gained recognition through his original and independent songs, including “Bas Ek Baar”, which was featured in the Indie Pop Song of the Year category at the Mirchi Music Awards. His work represents the growing space for independent singers outside mainstream Bollywood playback singing

Through his independent music and compositions, Soham continues to create songs that connect with listeners on a personal level.

Awards/recognition:
🎵 Mirchi Music Awards – Indie Pop Song of the Year selection for “Bas Ek Baar”
🎵 Recognised among the independent music artists featured by the Mirchi Music Awards.
